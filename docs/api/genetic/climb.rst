genetic/climb.hpp
=================

The lattice climb at the output. ``climb`` strengthens one gated FRETISH repair towards the original with the steps ``steps_towards`` proves, takes the first step whose result the predicate accepts, and stops in the first round where it accepts none. ``furthest_accepted`` bisects a ``within`` or ``for`` timing chain instead of stepping one tick at a time. ``order_steps`` puts the steps in slot order, by largest gain in syntactic similarity to the original, or under a fixed pseudo-random priority for each extra climb order. ``climb_gate`` is the output gate's own predicate behind a whole-specification realizability screen, and ``climb_repairs`` climbs every distinct repair concurrently and collects the results by index. Nothing draws from the ``RandomSource``.
