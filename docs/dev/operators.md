# Mutation and crossover operators

The breeding grammar on both front ends, what each operator is for, and the measurements behind each change to it.

## Crossover

Both crossovers follow AuRUS's `SpecificationCrossover` (`~/projects/tools/aurus/src/geneticalgorithm/SpecificationCrossover.java`, level ≥ 2) and its `FormulaUtils.replaceSubformula` / `combineSubformula`. Per side, *exactly one conjunct is drawn from each parent, uniformly and independently, and merged*: with probability 1/2 a subformula of the first is replaced by one from the second, otherwise the two are joined under a fresh binary operator. Every other slot is the first parent's, and no branch copies a field verbatim, so a crossover that fires always recombines.

The donor's slot is unrelated to the target's, so material moves between slots. The offspring keeps the first parent's shape, so only the in/out atoms (FRETISH) or signals (TLSF) must match; an equal-length guard would stop any individual that gained an assumption from breeding. The merge is written back in place rather than appended as AuRUS does, because slot *i* of a candidate must keep descending from slot *i* of the original (see "Removable guarantees").

Deleted and non-weakenable requirements are never target or donor. AuRUS's `≤ 2 temporal operators` rejection is dropped, the bloat cap doing that job. On TLSF a conjunct with no temporal subformula offers itself as its graft site, since INITIALLY, PRESET, REQUIRE and ASSERT are routinely propositional and crossover would otherwise be a no-op over most of a specification. FRETISH fields are propositional, so that path joins under ∧, ∨, →, ↔ rather than ∧, ∨, U, W.

The condition type and the scope are each taken whole from one parent on a coin after the timing's draw: two ungated draws per crossover. `test_generation_draw_sequence_is_pinned` holds the stream at 187 draws. The scope keeps its mode, which is sound because `crossover_specifications` refuses parents whose declared modes differ.

### Order crossover

`[genetic] p_order_crossover` (default 0, FRETISH only) replaces the graft with a crossover along the implication order (`include/genetic/order_crossover.hpp`). The graft writes one merged slot and copies the rest from the first parent, so two parents that fixed different slots never pass both fixes to one child. A slot-wise *join* implies both parents and a slot-wise *meet* is implied by both. Both come from `specification_join` and `specification_meet` (see "Order moves").

Realisability is closed under weakening, so the parents' verdicts decide which bound is worth building. A join of two realisable parents may be realisable, and then it beats both on maximality. A meet of two unrealisable parents may be realisable, and then it combines both parents' weakenings. The other cells are decided in advance and are never built: a meet of realisable parents is realisable and weaker than both, and a join with an unrealisable parent is unrealisable. A mixed pair takes *path relinking* instead. The child starts from the realisable parent R and adopts the unrealisable parent's value on a random non-empty subset of the slots where that value is proved strictly stronger. Each such child implies R and is implied by the join of the pair, which is unrealisable, so the realisability boundary lies on the path between them. A coin per slot picks the subset, and one slot is drawn outright when every coin falls tails.

A parent is realisable when its status objective reads `k_status_realizable`, which both gradings pay only for a realisable candidate. A lower score proves nothing, since the MRS walk also refuses a part for being ill-separated or undecided. A parent is unrealisable when `RealizabilityChecker::proved_unrealizable` finds a decided query whose assumptions include the parent's and whose guarantees are among the parent's. Every part the walk refused for being unrealisable leaves such a query behind, and the lookup asks no tool. The lookahead's queries are excluded, because which of them finish depends on timing; reading them would let the operator choice, and with it the draw stream, differ between two runs of one seed. `SpeculativeQueryScope` marks them, and the walk's own later cache hit on the same query admits the verdict. A pair with an unknown verdict takes the graft.

The verdicts are read after the partner draw, then the probability, then the operator's own draws. At 0, or on a pair without two verdicts, the arm draws nothing, and `test_new_arms_cost_no_draw_at_zero` pins both cases on the rendered breeding trace. A slot the rules cannot bound takes one parent's value on a coin, and the child is then a *partial* join or meet, which carries no implication. The original's assumption count does not reach `breed_offspring`, so the arm pairs as original slots the fewest assumptions any parent carries. Pairing an appended assumption by index is still sound, since every slot's bound is proved. The graft also runs where the arm yields nothing new: a bound the `Specification` constructor would merge, a relinking with no site, and a child equal to a parent. A join or meet equal to a parent is that parent, already scored, and a relinked child equal to the unrealisable parent is known unrealisable.

The bounds keep the simpler side wherever one propositional field implies the other, and the bloat cap handles the rest. A meet takes the union of the appended assumptions, so it grows fastest.

`order_crossover` in `run.json` counts, per kind, the crossovers `attempted`, those `proved` (a new child from a complete bound, or any new relinked child), and those whose offspring, after mutation and simplification, scored `realizable` in the generation that bred it. `--diagnostics` prints the same counts.

The default stays at 0. A seed-paired release measurement on 2026-10-31 compared 0 with 0.3 over `fsm`, `fsm-timing`, `fsm-combined`, `mode-arbiter`, `takeoff`, `liquid-mixer`, `valu3s-uc6` and `fsm-lmcps`, seeds 1 to 3. At 0.3, repairs rose from 701 to 789. Runs with a repair implying an ideal fell from 9 to 8, and ideal-implying repairs fell from 40 to 32, mostly on `fsm`. Total wall time rose from 230 s to 249 s, with `liquid-mixer` and `valu3s-uc6` about 40% slower. Of 85 joins, 64 were proved and 15 scored realisable. Of 316 meets, only 69 were proved and 3 realisable, because a meet of two unrealisable parents seldom has every slot bounded. Of 163 relinkings, 62 bred a child and 18 scored realisable. Three seeds cannot separate these differences from noise.

## Operator repairs of 2026-08-19

`experiments/2026-08-14-aurus-h2h/REPORT.md` measured defects in the grammar against 2,295 archived repairs; the seven fixes below are unconditional. The `[genetic] repaired_operators` key and the legacy paths were deleted, so restoring old behaviour means reverting the commit.

The evidence is `experiments/2026-08-20-ops-grammar` and `experiments/2026-08-20-ops-weakening` (720 paired cases, `REPORT.md` in the latter). With the weakening screen off, as now ships, the pooled endpoint is null on both paths and the pre-registered rule said do not flip. The decision rests instead on reachability: with the screen on, `minepump` reads 0/20 under the legacy grammar against 12/20, while `humanoid-531`, which builds much larger formulae, loses runs to the repairs. Sweep O and the `ops-*`/`opswk-*` profiles are retired; those archives reproduce only through their vendored `scripts/`.

`src/prop_formula/simplify.cpp` carries ungated folds: `G G φ → G φ`, `F F φ → F φ`, the U/W/R self-joins, and the boolean constants through every temporal operator (`φ W false ≡ G φ` and `false R ψ ≡ G ψ` being the two that do not annihilate). The constant folds exist because the `Constant` monotone rewrite left spellings such as `G(false)` standing, and 43.7% of formulae reaching `ltlsynt` mentioned a constant. They do not change wall time (paired A/B over 33 cases, sign p = 1.000), never fire on FRETISH, and stay because a guarantee folding to `true` is gutted whether or not anything notices. Both ops campaigns ran with them in both arms and attribute nothing to them.

- **An atom can grow.** `mutate_atom_formula` draws a third move beside rename and negate: graft a drawn anchor, with drawn polarity, onto the atom under a connective. Otherwise guarding a positive literal takes three chained draws; without the polarity draw `minepump`'s ideal `high_water & !methane` stayed at 1 of 20. An empty atom pool keeps the two-move draw.
- **The temporal path can preserve a connective.** `mutate_temporal` case (3) has an arm keeping the node's kind and mutating its children, so an implication is not reachable only to be destroyed.
- **An assumption obliges an input.** `tlsf_add_assumption` draws the consequent from the inputs alone, since an assumption obliging an output can be defeated by the system withholding it; the guard may draw an output. The consequent's modality is drawn from {F, X, nothing}, and a guard identical to its consequent has its polarity flipped.
- **Sections are not sides.** An INITIALLY or PRESET slot draws from its own signals, takes only the propositional rewrite, and accepts a donor only from its counterpart section, as Basic TLSF requires. The atom pool is per-section; the bloat cap's baseline is not.
- **One occurrence gives way.** TLSF crossover's graft replaces the first occurrence of the chosen subformula, not every one.
- **The FRETISH graft site is uniform.** `replace_subformula` and `combine_subformula` draw the site uniformly, since a coin per node reaches the k-th with probability 2⁻ᵏ and grafts nowhere with 2⁻ⁿ. `mutate_atom_name` has its TLSF twin's distinctness guard.

The bloat cap keeps its specification-wide baseline. A per-section baseline tightens it where a weakening needs room, raising `arbiter-aurus`'s drop rate from 1.6% to 9.4% and costing 9 of its 10 ideal-implying runs. Watch for a filter tightening that fights an operator loosening.

`tlsf_mutate` draws its slot before the atom pool, and TLSF `cross_side` draws its target before testing the donor pool, because the pool follows the slot's section. The FRETISH goldens do not cover the TLSF path, so reordering either is silent.

## Implication in the TLSF grammar

`pick_binary_kind` (`src/tlsf/mutation.cpp`) draws from {∧, ∨, →, U, R, W}. Without `Implies` (Brizzio's negation normal form fragment has none), `ltl2dba-r-2`, `ltl2dba-theta-2` and `ltl2dba27` are unreachable, each ideal replacing a root `<->` with `->`, which `mutate_temporal` case (3) reaches once the draw includes it. The FRETISH twins in `src/genetic/mutation.cpp` and `src/genetic/crossover.cpp` draw `{And, Or, Implies, Iff}`, so this is TLSF-only. `Iff` stays out: the defect was that a biconditional could not be weakened, and nothing supports manufacturing them.

`pick_connective_kind` draws `next_index(5)` over {U, W, ∧, ∨, →} for case (2d), which grafts an anchor onto the mutated child at an atom or unary node. `Implies` there makes `p -> X phi`, the shape of every minimal guarantee weakening, one draw away where a guard is introduced. The anchor goes first, giving `anchor -> inner`; `Release` is left to `pick_binary_kind`. The `connective_implies` key was removed, so no key restores the old TLSF stream.

## Monotone rewrites

`monotone_rewrite` (`src/genetic/monotone.cpp`, `include/genetic/monotone.hpp`) serves both front ends under `[mutation] p_monotone` (`Config::p_monotone`, default 0.25). On TLSF it is a rewrite arm offered ahead of the temporal and propositional ones; on FRETISH it is offered inside `p_response` and `p_trigger`, so it is inert unless one is above 0. It lives in the genetic layer because `src/tlsf/mutation.cpp` includes `genetic/mutation.hpp`, never the reverse, which is also why `draw_literal` is in `include/genetic/mutation.hpp`. `Weaken` returns a formula the parent implies, `Strengthen` one implying the parent.

The general rewriters leave the implication order, changing AST shape 93.6% of the time. Rescored through PEREDUR's maximality filter over the shared families, PEREDUR reads `best_relation` incomparable on 37.5% of runs against AuRUS's 22.7% (`maximality_rescore` in `experiments/2026-08-14-aurus-h2h/PROVENANCE.json`, script `scripts/aurus_maxscore.py`). Two of AuRUS's three mutation visitors are monotone, against which 0.25 is a conservative first value; no campaign has tuned it.

**Polarity is what makes the guarantee hold through nesting.** A weaker subformula weakens the whole only at positive occurrence, so `Not` and an `Implies` antecedent flip direction and an `Iff` child is not a rewrite site. Site and rule are drawn uniformly over nodes with determinate polarity and rules applicable there. A new rule goes into `rules_at` or `extra_rules_at` under its node kind *and* its direction; a misfiled one fails `test_monotone_rewrite_direction_holds`.

The FRETISH arm applies this to the lowering as a three-entry table in `src/genetic/mutation.cpp`. `scope_wrap` and both condition wrappers place `body` positively, so the response takes the requirement's direction, flipped under an `only` scope (its lowering applies the dual wrapper to `negated_timing_body`, putting the response under a `Not`) and sat out under `after n ticks`, where it occurs at both polarities. The condition takes the flipped direction under a continual condition type and is sat out under a trigger, whose rising edge `(!c & Xc)` holds both polarities. `test/genetic/monotone_arm_tests.cpp` (ctest `peredur_tests.fretish_monotone`) checks the table against a solver; dropping any of the three guards fails it.

On TLSF the direction is a fair coin: a search that only weakens guarantees and strengthens assumptions cannot recover from an ancestor that overshot the ideal, and the arm's purpose is comparability. The FRETISH arm follows the requirement's mutation direction, as its timing, condition-type and scope arms do.

The probability is read before the RNG is drawn, so at 0 the arm costs no draw. `test_zero_probability_costs_no_draw` (`test/tlsf/monotone_tests.cpp`) pins TLSF; `test_new_arms_cost_no_draw_at_zero` (`test/genetic/determinism_tests.cpp`) pins FRETISH by comparing rendered draw traces, since a replacement rewrite can draw equally often down another branch. `golden_config()` pins the key at 0, and the key is in the "Config vintage" note in `docs/dev/config-and-provenance.md`.

`AddOperand` and `Constant`, the two rules sound at every node, are offered everywhere. Limiting `AddOperand` to `And` and `Or` would leave `Constant` as the only move at a literal, while every assumption-shaped ideal is a disjunction of literals. The connective comes from the direction, not the node kind.

Five extra rules fill the `Release`, `Next` and `Iff` menus: `phi R psi` weakens to `psi` and strengthens to `G psi`; `X phi` weakens to `F phi` and strengthens to `G phi`; `a <-> b` strengthens to `a & b` or `!a & !b` on a coin. `rules_at` appends `extra_rules_at`'s result. Both widenings are unconditional, so the signature is `monotone_rewrite(formula, direction, atoms, random_source)`. `test_monotone_rewrite_direction_holds` includes `Release` and `Next` subjects, checking every rule against `black`.

## Cloned assumptions

`tlsf_add_assumption` emits at most 7 nodes, while assumption-shaped ideals are far larger (`gyro-var2`'s is about 29). Before the clone arm, 112 emitted `gyro-var2` repairs appended only 6 assumptions, every one template-shaped. Under `[tlsf.mutation] p_clone_assumption` (default 0.25) the operator instead appends a copy of a live ASSUME conjunct for later mutation to edit. PEREDUR's crossover draws one conjunct per side and cannot union subsets as AuRUS's level-1 crossover does, so the move belongs to mutation.

Only ASSUME is drawn from, since copying INITIALLY or REQUIRE into ASSUME changes its meaning. Tombstones are skipped, and with nothing live the template stands in. The template keeps the majority of the draw as the only form that introduces a new fairness property.

The probability is read before the `RandomSource` is touched, so at 0 it costs no draw; the key is in the "Config vintage" note.

## Assumption construction

On both paths an appended assumption obliges an *input* literal, and only its guard may draw an output. An assumption obliging an output is one the system defeats by never raising its own signal, and well-separation passes the guarded form `G(<lit> -> F <output>)`, because an environment that never raises the guard satisfies it. A guard equal to the obliged literal is negated at no extra draw, which keeps the tautology `G(l -> F l)` out. With no input, nothing is appended: FRETISH skips the operator's draw, and TLSF can still clone. The FRETISH path (`add_assumption` in `src/genetic/mutation.cpp`) caught up with TLSF on 2026-09-24, which moved its draw stream; `docs/dev/config-and-provenance.md` records the break.

The campaign `experiments/2026-08-26-assumption-reach` (sweep U) tested five keys. Its primary is null (exact McNemar p = 1.0000).

Five keys are removed. `[tlsf.mutation] max_assumption_width` (a body of up to n literals joined by `&` and `|`) and `p_bare_assumption` (an unconditional `F body` in place of `G F body`) each had one reach: `lift` repaired 3 of 12 under `reach-l`, and `lily11` rose from 5 to 11 of 12. Both stayed at their no-op defaults and were removed on 2026-10-30, so an appended body is one input literal and an unconditional assumption is always `G F`. `[tlsf.crossover] p_union_assumption` is unreachable by construction: `gyro-var2`'s ideal is a conjunct of `gyro-var1`'s specification and vice versa, material in a sibling no run sees, and arming it displaced clone-and-edit. `[tlsf.mutation] p_burst_continue` measured a loss (p = 0.0226), PEREDUR already mutating more aggressively than AuRUS's `Poisson(1)`; mutation applies exactly one edit. `[tlsf.mutation] p_remove_assumption` had no reach attributed. Sweep U and the `assumption-reach` profile are retired.

## Timing donation

The extremes of the FRETISH timing order move only into a timing the specification donates. `collect_timing_pool` gathers distinct timings from both lists, skipping tombstones, and `donated_candidates` (`src/genetic/mutation.cpp`) builds the candidates: a quantified donor lends its tick count as `for n ticks`, Immediately and NextTimepoint lend themselves. With no donor the extreme is unchanged.

Everything in the set lies strictly between Always and Eventually, so the same candidates strengthen one and weaken the other through the shared `move_off_extreme`. A donor's kind is never taken: `within n` respells one count and `after n` is not comparable to Always.

Always also takes a second list, `donated_to_always`: `until s` for every stop `s` that a live `until` or `before` timing carries. Always implies `until s` for any `s`, but `until s` does not imply Eventually, so these candidates stay out of the shared set. They are appended after it, and `until` sorts after every other kind, so a specification with no stop timing builds the same list as before and a draw lands on the same element. In the other direction `until s` strengthens to Always, the one kind above it, and `before s` is incomparable with every kind; both weaken only through their stop.

No invented constant: a hard-coded `for 10 ticks` weakening of Always produced a degenerate `fsm` repair, and freezing Always (`f4968ab`) left the timing of every Always guarantee immobile, 7 of the 9 across the three FRETISH input specs.

A weaker guarantee timing helps realizability and costs nothing on similarity, so under NSGA-II a gutted timing sits on the front; that wants a campaign. `maximality_demotion` moves such a candidate down a front where a stronger realizable one is in the pool (see "Maximality demotion" in `docs/dev/algorithm.md`).

`move_off_extreme` reads the pool before touching the `RandomSource`, so an empty pool costs no draw, and no golden specification has an Always guarantee.

## Scope-aware timing moves

Every timing step above is an edge of the Global order, and a scope changes which edges hold. `timing_implies` (`src/requirement.cpp`) states the order for each scope, measured with SPOT over 14 timings, 8 scopes and both condition types (2,912 ordered pairs, condition type moving none). `test_timing_order_is_pinned` re-derives every edge and non-edge over 15 timings with tick counts 0 to 4, and `timing_implies_exact` in `proofs/Fretish/Cpp.lean` proves the order exact at every tick count. That proof found `after 0 ⇒ next`, which the 14 measured timings left out because none was `after 0`. Under the only scopes it reverses to `next ⇒ after 0`.

The After scope keeps the Global order. In, NotIn and Before drop the edges from `next`, `within n` and `after n` into Eventually, because the bounded lowering discharges those three when the interval closes (`boundary | X r`), while a bounded Eventually (`!boundary U r`) is not discharged. The only scopes negate the obligation through `dual_timing`, so their order is the Global one reversed, with `eventually` also above `before s`. In that reversed order `until s` is not below Always, and under OnlyIn and OnlyAfter neither `for n` nor `next` is.

`mutate_timing` takes the requirement's scope. Under an only scope it runs the opposite operator, which is a step in the asked direction once the order is reversed. Any step that is not an edge of the scope's order returns the timing unchanged. The step is drawn before the check, so the draw count is the same under every scope, and under Global and After no step is refused (an `assert` holds this). The two moves refused under In, NotIn and Before are the Eventually branch of `weaken_within_timing` and a strengthened Eventually drawing a donated `next`. `test_timing_mutation_moves_in_direction_under_every_scope` checks each move against SPOT, and the Global-only operator fails it at its first weakening under In.

## Removable guarantees

`p_remove_guarantee` (default 0.05, matching `p_add_assumption`) deletes one FRETISH guarantee or one TLSF conjunct from PRESET, ASSERT and GUARANTEE. Some ideals need it: amba, full-arbiter, load-balancer, prioritized-arbiter and round-robin-arbiter have only `drop-*` ideals. `lint-ideals`' `reachable` check asserts a guarantee side may shrink, never grow, to a floor of one.

A deleted guarantee is *tombstoned in place, never erased* (`Requirement::m_removed`, `tlsf::SectionEntry::m_removed`), because specifications pair by position: `average_timing_similarity` and both semantic-similarity folds zip by index and crossovers write back into the source slot.

The flag is in `operator<`, `operator==` and `hash`, so a deleted guarantee cannot inherit a live one's cached fitness. Every requirement-list reader skips tombstones: LTL lowering, status components and the MRS index map, vacuity, well-separation, the bloat cap, `all_implied_by_some`, the timing, mutation and simplification pools, MUC extraction and the guarantee-part split. Crossover never draws a tombstone, so removal is mutation's alone. `tlsf::SectionEntry` converts implicitly from `Formula` but not back, so a new read site fails to compile.

`to_json` and `tlsf::write` omit tombstones, so a written repair carries no flag to misread.

The probability is tested before the RNG is drawn, so at 0 it costs no draw. Archived configs predate the key, so reproduce them with `p_remove_guarantee = 0.0` (see "Config vintage"). `golden_config()` pins the key.

Deletion helps realizability and costs nothing on similarity, so under NSGA-II a gutted candidate sits on the front. A sweep measuring that needs a `p_remove_guarantee = 0` arm as its control. `maximality_demotion` reads a tombstone as a `true` guarantee, so it demotes the gutted candidate wherever the intact one is realizable and in the pool.

## Order moves

`include/genetic/order_moves.hpp` decides the implication order over FRETISH specifications by rule. It asks no solver and draws no random numbers. The order crossover (see "Crossover") builds on its joins and meets, the output climb below uses `steps_towards` and `timing_chain`, and the rest is groundwork for a mutation direction gated on status.

`classify_move(parent, child)` reads the child against its parent over the whole `A -> G` formula. The child is *stronger* when each live guarantee of the parent is implied by one of the child's and each live assumption of the child by one of the parent's, and *weaker* in the converse case. A requirement implies another when the two lower identically, wherever they sit, or when `requirement_implies` proves it of the pair in one slot. Appended assumptions do not pair by index, so the identical-lowering test is what matches them, and dropping the first of two appended assumptions reads as a strengthening. Several moved slots combine through the same test, and anything unproved reads as Incomparable.

`requirement_join` returns a requirement implying both inputs (their *join*), and `requirement_meet` one both inputs imply (their *meet*). An input proved to imply the other is returned unchanged. Otherwise each field moves along its own order. The response takes `p & q` for a join and `p | q` for a meet, swapped under an only scope and refused under `after n ticks`, where the response occurs at both polarities. A continual condition takes the dual connective. Two trigger conditions must be equivalent, except that a join of two triggers turns continual over `c | c'`. The timing is the weakest (join) or strongest (meet) candidate the scope's timing order admits, enumerated up to one tick past the inputs' largest count. Where one propositional field implies the other the simpler is kept, so `x & (x | y)` stays `x`. Every result is checked with `requirement_implies` against both inputs, and an unproved pair returns nothing. A locked requirement joins only an equal one.

`specification_join` and `specification_meet` pair the original's assumption slots and the guarantee slots by index, and return each slot's result or nothing in a `SlotwiseBound`. The assumption side takes the opposite bound. A tombstone reads as `true`, so a join keeps a guarantee live if either input does and a meet removes it if either does. A join keeps only the appended assumptions both inputs carry, and a meet keeps their union. `assemble` builds the specification from a complete bound, and refuses one whose slots the `Specification` constructor would merge.

`steps_towards(current, original)` lists the strengthenings of `current` that move it towards the original. A guarantee slot moves towards `requirement_join` of itself and the original's slot, which also covers a slot that moved sideways. An original assumption slot moves towards `requirement_meet`. Each field in which the slot differs from its target gives one step, kept where `classify_move` proves it Stronger. Some field always qualifies: the rule chain that proved the target ends with a one-field link into the slot. A tombstoned guarantee is restored with the fields it was removed with, and each appended assumption can be dropped. `timing_chain` lists, in order, the `within n` and `for n` timings between two comparable timings, which a climb can bisect instead of stepping one tick at a time.

`test/genetic/order_moves_tests.cpp` (ctest `peredur_tests.order_moves`) pins each rule, and sends every classified move, join, meet and step over a sampled family to SPOT. The propositional check behind `requirement_implies` is public as `formula_implies`, and the batched SPOT oracle lives in `test/ltl_oracle.hpp`.

## Blame-targeted slot choice

`mutate_specification` draws the slot it rewrites uniformly over the live weakenable slots. Weakening a guarantee outside every unrealizable core cannot make the specification realizable, so that draw is often wasted. `[mutation] blame_targeted_slots` (`Config::blame_targeted_slots`, default off, FRETISH only) narrows the list by the parent's status. An unrealizable parent draws from the guarantees its MRS walk refused plus every live weakenable assumption, since strengthening any assumption can help. A realizable parent draws from the slots that differ from the original, counting an appended assumption as differing, because only those can step back towards it. An empty list keeps the full one. `targeted_mutation_slots` (`src/genetic/mutation.cpp`) builds the list.

The refused set comes from the walk that scored the parent. `status_score_mrs` reports the parts it refused, and `specification_status` maps them from walk positions to `m_guarantees` slots through `live_indices`, so a tombstone shifts nothing. The query sequence and the score are unchanged. The fitness cache stores the set beside the objectives (`ObjectiveRecord`), and every path that fills `Scored::objectives` also fills `Scored::refused_guarantees`, so a cache hit restores what a fresh walk gave. The set is empty wherever the walk did not grade the candidate: a failed component, an unsatisfiable whole specification, the AuRUS ladder, or a run with no status objective. The split scoring path runs the walk behind a failed component anyway and discards its set to match the serial path. An unrealizable parent with an empty set therefore keeps the full list. TLSF reports nothing, since its walk runs over conjuncts split out of whole sections and no slot names them.

`breed_offspring` hands mutation a `ParentContext` per offspring: the parent's status, whether it reached `k_status_realizable`, its refused guarantees, whether crossover fired on the offspring, and a pointer to the original. The original reaches breeding by pointer from `run_fretish_repair` through `run_generations`, `evolve_generation` and `GenerationContext::m_original`, and is never copied. A crossover offspring keeps the full list, because the first parent's context is only an estimate of it.

The arm filters the list the single `next_index` indexes, so it adds no draw, though it changes which slot the draw picks. `test_slot_targeting_costs_no_draw_when_off` (`test/genetic/determinism_tests.cpp`) checks that a context changes nothing with the key off and that the slot draw's bound shrinks with it on. `golden_config()` pins the key off. `n_targeted_mutations` in `run.json` counts the rewrites drawn from a list narrower than the full one. No campaign has measured the arm against uniform choice yet.

## Output climb

`[filters] climb_output` (FRETISH only, off by default) strengthens each gated repair towards the original before the final filters. The implication filter can only choose among the repairs the search found, and the climb makes stronger ones. `include/genetic/climb.hpp` holds it, and it draws no random numbers.

`climb(S, O)` takes the `steps_towards(S, O)` of the order moves above, in the order `climb_order` sets, and tries each until one passes. A step passes when its result passes the output gate's own predicate, `passes_output_gate` on the run's grading and status objective. The climb then moves to that result and lists the steps again, and it stops in the first round where no step passes. Every accepted step is proved a strengthening, so the result implies S by rule. A result the climb has already visited is never taken twice, which ends the climb even where two specifications are each proved stronger than the other.

`climb_gate` puts a whole-specification realizability query in front of the gate. Under MRS grading the gate's walk asks every guarantee subset after the first part a step changed, and one `ltlsynt` query on the whole settles most refusals. The screen rejects a candidate only on a decided unrealizable verdict, which the gate would also reject. Its decided verdicts are also the entries `rule_subsumed_verdict` reads (see "Cache keys" in `docs/dev/performance.md`): a step refused in one round is stronger in the next round than the refused candidate, so the memo refuses it again without an exec.

A timing step on a `within n` or `for n` chain is bisected. `furthest_accepted` tries the destination first, then binary-searches the `timing_chain` entries before it for the furthest that passes. The entries strengthen along the chain, and a strengthening of an unrealizable specification is unrealizable, so the passing entries form a prefix. The search therefore stops where a walk of one tick at a time stops, at one query plus log₂ of the chain's length instead of one query per tick.

Realisability is closed under weakening: a controller for S satisfies every S' that S implies. The realisable specifications reachable from O therefore form a *down-set*, and the repairs worth reporting are its maximal elements. A climb that stops is maximal over everything its step set can reach. Suppose no single step from S passes, but some T strictly stronger than S and implied by `join(S, O)` is realisable. A chain of single steps leads from S up to T, and T implies the chain's first element, so that element is realisable too, which contradicts the premise. Compound moves therefore add nothing. The guarantee covers only the step set: a stronger realisable specification reachable only through a propositional rewrite outside the steps stays out of reach. It also covers realisability alone. The gate asks well-separation too, which is not closed under weakening, and a timeout reads as a refusal, so a climb over the gate's predicate can stop below a point the argument promises.

The climb is the *grow* step of maximal-satisfiable-subset extraction, as in MARCO, over a product of field orders where MARCO grows over a set of clauses. The down-set has many incomparable maximal elements, and the step order decides which one a climb reaches. `climb_order = "slot"` (the default) tries the steps in `steps_towards` order: assumption slots, then guarantee slots, then the appended assumptions. `"similarity"` tries the largest gain in syntactic similarity to the original first, the fitness function's syntactic term, as a simplex pivot rule picks the steepest edge. `climb_orders` (default 1) climbs each repair that many times. Each climb after the first orders the steps by a fixed SplitMix64 priority over the step's slot, side, kind and field, and every distinct result goes on to the implication filter, which enumerates maximal elements the way MARCO does.

`climb_repairs` climbs the distinct gated repairs concurrently and collects the results by index, so the output does not depend on how the gate's queries interleave. A run whose gate verdicts do not time out is reproducible for a fixed seed. Within `finish_search` the climbed repairs replace the gated ones before deduplication. The implication stream already holds the accumulated repairs, so it receives the climbed ones as well, and each climbed result implies the repair it came from and removes it. Every climbed result passed the gate, so the driver accumulates it like any other gate-passing candidate: a file under `accumulated/` named with the generation after the search's last, and an `index.tsv` row stamped when the climb ended. Before 2026-11-11 the climb pushed its results into the stream with no file, so `maximal.tsv` and the anytime curves left out every climbed repair: in the `2026-11-09-fretish-order-pooled` cores, only-climb's listings held 3,529 specifications against 11,593 in its output. A gated repair stays in the output only where the stream could not prove that its climbed result implies it. The filter report gains a `final/climb` row, and `run.json` reports the work under `climb` (`climbed`, `steps_tried`, `steps_accepted`, `queries`, `ltlsynt_execs`). With the key off, `finish_search` takes the path it took before and writes the same repairs.

On the eight small FRETISH examples (`fsm`, `fsm-timing`, `fsm-combined`, `mode-arbiter`, `takeoff`, `liquid-mixer`, `valu3s-uc6`, `fsm-lmcps`), seeds 1 to 3, the default config and a release build (`peredur --seed N --diagnostics`, the climb arms adding `[filters] climb_output = true`), the climb cut `n_repairs` from 701 to 468 in slot order and to 466 in similarity order. `implies_ideal` held at 9 of the 21 runs whose example has ideals, in every arm. The repairs implying an ideal (`compare`'s equivalent plus strictly stronger) rose from 24 to 36 on `fsm-timing` and from 10 to 15 on `takeoff`, and fell from 6 to 5 on `fsm`. Slot order accepted 3,767 steps over 8,617 queries, of which 1,771 reached `ltlsynt`; the memo, its rules and the stored controllers settled the rest. Similarity order asked 8,965 queries and 1,551 execs with the same `implies_ideal` and one or two fewer ideal-implying repairs on `fsm-timing` and `takeoff`, so these runs do not separate the two orders. Median wall time rose by at most 2.2x, on `fsm-timing` (5.3 s to 11.5 s), on a machine other jobs were using. With the key off, all 24 runs wrote repairs byte-identical to a build of `038ccc4`, and a repeated climb run wrote the same repairs.

## Status-gated mutation direction

Every directed move weakens the specification: a guarantee weakens and an assumption strengthens. A search that only weakens cannot step back from an ancestor that went past the ideal. `[mutation] direction_gated` (`Config::direction_gated_mutation`, default off, FRETISH only) aims the move by the parent's status instead. The gate opens only where the `ParentContext` carries a status and crossover did not fire, since the first parent's status says little about a crossed offspring. Realisability is closed under weakening, so an unrealisable parent should weaken and a realisable one should strengthen, which keeps the population near the realisability boundary where the maximal repairs lie. The gate reads the status score: 1.0 counts as realisable and anything lower as unrealisable.

An unrealisable parent moves as it does without the gate. A realisable parent skips `add_assumption` and `remove_guarantee`, both weakenings, before their draws. Its directed moves come from `steps_towards(parent, original)`: a field stepped towards the original, a tombstone restored or an appended assumption dropped, each proved stronger by `classify_move`. One `next_index` picks over the steps and the rewritable slots together, each counted once. A parent far from the original therefore mostly steps back, and one at the original mostly rewrites sideways. A chosen slot is rewritten with every directed arm at 0 (`p_timing`, `p_condition_type`, `p_scope` and `p_monotone`), so the rewrite is free and no strengthening goes past the original. The arms at 0 still take the `next_real` draws they always take.

A *free rewrite* is a response, condition or stop rewrite that did not take the monotone arm, and it has no direction. `mutate_specification` sorts its result with `classify_move` against the parent. A result proved to move the forbidden way, stronger than an unrealisable parent or weaker than a realisable one, returns the parent, and deduplication then removes it. Redrawing would also work, but would move the draw stream further. An incomparable result is kept with probability `[mutation] p_sideways` (default 1). The share is read before the `RandomSource` is touched and is drawn only for an incomparable result, so at 0 or 1 it costs no draw. Directed moves are never sorted, since each moves the allowed way by construction and the rules would read some of them as incomparable.

Off, the gate costs no draw and changes nothing: `test_direction_gate_costs_no_draw_when_off` (`test/genetic/determinism_tests.cpp`) checks that a context in either state leaves the stream and the offspring alone, that a realisable parent skips the add-assumption draw with the gate on, and that `p_sideways` at 0 and at 1 draw the same stream. `golden_config()` pins both keys. The `direction_gate` suite checks over 400 seeds that an unrealisable parent never yields a child proved stronger and a realisable one never a child proved weaker. `direction_gate` in `run.json` counts the forbidden and refused-sideways rewrites returned to the parent, and the steps taken.

## Distance-dependent step size

The status objective under MRS grading is the fraction of guarantee parts the walk keeps, so `1 - status` measures how far a candidate is from realisable. A parent far from it needs a large weakening, and a parent near it should take single edges, which do not overshoot. `[mutation] step_scaling` (`Config::step_scaling`, a share in [0, 1], default 0, FRETISH only) multiplies the probability of each *large move* by `1 + step_scaling * (1 - 2 * status)`, capped at 1. At `step_scaling = 1` a parent at status 0 takes large moves at twice their usual rate, one at status 0.5 at their usual rate, and a realisable one never. `step_scale` (`src/genetic/mutation.cpp`) reads the key before the context, so at 0 nothing is read and nothing is drawn. A crossover offspring, or a parent without a status, is unscaled.

Three kinds of move count as large. Removing a guarantee scales the `p_remove_guarantee` rate. A timing on Always (weakening) or Eventually (strengthening) moves only into a donated timing and has no single edge to take instead, so the timing arm's `p_timing` rate scales for that requirement. A tick-count step chooses between its single edge and its jumps: `for n` weakens to `for n-1` or `for n/2`, `within n` to `within n+1`, `within 2n` or Eventually, and the strengthenings mirror these, with `within n` to `after n-1` counted as a jump. Under a scale one `next_real` against the jumps' unscaled share times the factor picks the class, and a `next_bool` picks between two jumps. At a factor of 1 every share is the unscaled one, though the stream differs from the unscaled `next_bool` or `next_index(3)`.

`[mutation] bisect_steps` (`Config::bisect_steps`, default off, FRETISH only) acts under the direction gate. A realisable parent's timing step from `steps_towards` goes all the way to its target's timing, which on a `within n` or `for n` chain can cross many ticks. With the key on, `bisect_step` moves it to the midpoint of `timing_step_chain`, rounding towards the destination: `within 8` with an original at `within 2` steps to `within 5`. A chain with no intermediate entry, or a midpoint that would merge two entries or that `classify_move` cannot prove Stronger, keeps the full step. If the midpoint is realisable its offspring can step again, so over generations the population bisects the chain as the output climb's `furthest_accepted` does within one climb. Bisection replaces a step's result and keeps the list's length, so it draws nothing.

`test_step_scaling_costs_no_draw_when_off` (`test/genetic/determinism_tests.cpp`) checks that at 0 a context in either state leaves the stream and the offspring alone, that bisection under the gate leaves the stream alone, and that the scale, on, changes some tick-count draw. `golden_config()` pins both keys. The `step_size` suite checks each large move's rate at status 0 and 1, the crossover exemption, and the midpoint. `run.json` reports `step_scaling` (`scaled`, `large`, `small`) and `direction_gate.bisected`.

On the eight small FRETISH examples (`fsm`, `fsm-timing`, `fsm-combined`, `mode-arbiter`, `takeoff`, `liquid-mixer`, `valu3s-uc6`, `fsm-lmcps`), seeds 1 to 3, the default config and a release build (`peredur --seed N --diagnostics`), `step_scaling = 1` cut `n_repairs` from 700 to 635, and `bisect_steps` on top of `direction_gated` moved it from 643 (the gate alone) to 654. `implies_ideal` held at 9 of the 21 runs whose example has ideals in all four arms. The repairs implying an ideal (`compare`'s equivalent plus strictly stronger) summed to 40 with both keys off, 41 under scaling, 34 under the gate and 38 under the gate with bisection. Scaling rose from 10 to 13 on `takeoff` and fell from 6 to 4 on `fsm`, and bisection restored `fsm-timing` from 20 to 24. The scale applied to 17,235 mutations, of which 841 took a large move and 583 a single tick-count edge, and bisection moved 144 of 1,707 steps. Median wall time stayed within 0.2 s of the off arm's 3.0 s. Three seeds cannot separate these from noise, so both defaults stay off until a campaign decides them.
