# fretish-ablation

This plan is pre-registered. It fixes the question, design, corpus, tests and decision rule before any row of the campaign exists.

## Question

On FRETISH, the structured-English requirement language of the Formal Requirements Elicitation Tool (FRET), does *directed mutation* of the three ordered requirement fields find repairs more often or sooner than a *uniform redraw* of the same fields? The three fields are timing, condition type and scope.

Directed mutation moves each field along its *implication order* in the requirement's direction. Guarantees weaken and assumptions strengthen, and counts and stops are drawn from the spec's *donation pool*. A uniform redraw picks each field from the values the spec makes available and ignores the order.

The config key is `[mutation] ordered_fields = "directed" | "uniform"`, and `"directed"` is the shipping default. The *monotone rewrite* of condition and response (`p_monotone`) stays directed in both arms. The ablation therefore isolates the three ordered fields and nothing else.

## Design

Sweep O has two levels, `directed` and `uniform`, generated by `scripts/gen_configs.py`. Every other key sits at its shipping default, pinned with `--pin-vintage`.

The budget is identical to the paper-rerun TLSF (Temporal Logic Synthesis Format) campaign, so rows read directly against the paper's evaluation tables. That means `nsga2-apportion` selection under the non-dominated sorting genetic algorithm II (NSGA-II), `mrs` status grading, weights 0.1/0.2/0.7 and the log metric. Weakening is off. Each run terminates at 1000 individuals with population 100 and `parallel = 1`.

The runner caps each run at 7200 s. No `max_wall_s` is set, so the runner cap is the *censoring point*, as in paper-rerun. Each cell runs 30 seeds (0–29) at 16 concurrent runs per host.

Seeds 0–14 run on av2 and seeds 15–29 on av3. Both arms of a (subject, seed) pair run on the same host inside one campaign, so the arms are interleaved and wall time is comparable pair by pair. av2 starts only after the hand-run overlap sampling chain there finishes.

## Corpus

The run profile `fretish-ablation` holds 20 subjects. Eight are whole specs: `takeoff`, `fsm`, `fsm-timing`, `fsm-combined`, `fsm-lmcps`, `liquid-mixer`, `mode-arbiter` and `valu3s-uc6`.

The other 12 are *unrealisable cores* of the specs too large to repair whole, each repaired as its own spec. `rad` contributes 10: `rad-core-1-18`, `-10`, `-12-18`, `-17-18`, `-33`, `-45`, `-55`, `-61`, `-65` and `-68`. `lift-plus-cruise-mini` contributes `lpc-mini-core1` (8 core guarantees plus the non-weakenable speed-limit background). `lift-plus-cruise-full` contributes `lpc-full-core1` (12 core guarantees plus background). `scripts/make_core_specs.py` generates every core example.

`ventilator-controller` is excluded. Its subsets of size 4 or less hold no core, and its size-5 screen is still running.

Cores carry no ideals, and `valu3s-uc6` has none either. `mode-arbiter`'s single ideal is unreachable by construction. `implies_ideal` is therefore readable on 6 subjects only: `takeoff`, `fsm`, `fsm-timing`, `fsm-combined`, `fsm-lmcps` and `liquid-mixer`.

The campaign is 20 subjects × 2 arms × 30 seeds, or 1200 runs.

## Endpoints and tests

The endpoints match the paper's evaluation table.

**Found.** Whether a repair exists at 10 s, at 100 s and at the cap. Found is *anytime*: it is read from the score phase's curves over accumulated candidates, and censored runs count.

**Finished.** Whether the run ended before the 7200 s cap killed it.

**Wall time.** Median and mean per cell.

**Repairs.** Maximal and All repair counts.

The primary endpoint is found at the cap. It is tested by McNemar's exact test on the 600 (subject, seed) pairs, two-sided, at alpha 0.05.

The secondary endpoints are found at 10 s and at 100 s under the same test, and paired wall time by the Wilcoxon signed-rank test on pairs where both arms finished. `implies_ideal` is reported descriptively on the 6 subjects with reachable ideals.

The reading rests on the primary test alone. Directed wins if McNemar's p < 0.05 and more discordant pairs favour directed. Uniform wins under the symmetric condition. Any other outcome is null and is reported as null. No other decision rests on this campaign.

## Reporting

Results are reported per subject first. The cores are then aggregated by parent (`rad`, `lpc-mini`, `lpc-full`), and finally the whole corpus is reported overall. Every wall-time comparison is paired.

## Cost

Calibration at seed 0 on av3, directed arm only, puts the campaign at 98–177 core-hours. `lpc-full-core1` sets the spread, at 2500 s to the full 7200 s cap per run. With a 30% margin that is 4–7 h wall on both hosts, or 8–14 h on av3 alone. The estimate excludes the score phase.

## Follow-up

Once the `ventilator-controller` screen yields confirmed cores, a second campaign runs them under the same profile and sweep from this campaign's provenance tag. Its rows are appended per subject, and no row of this campaign is re-run.

A null here is a usable result. It would mean the implication order the operators encode buys nothing on FRETISH that a uniform draw over the same values does not.
