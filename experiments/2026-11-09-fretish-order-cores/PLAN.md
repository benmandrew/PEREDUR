# FRETISH order-directed search on rad's cores

Registered 2026-11-09, before launch.

## Question

`2026-11-05-fretish-order` ran whole `rad` under `base`, `full` and random `base`, 10 seeds each. One `full` run found a repair and the other 29 found none, every run ending well inside the 7200 s cap. Earlier FRETISH campaigns (`2026-09-29-fretish-ablation`, `2026-10-06-fretish-rerun`) repaired `rad` one unrealisable core at a time instead. This campaign does the same for the order-directed search: it asks RQ1 and RQ2 of `2026-11-05-fretish-order/PLAN.md` again on the ten cores.

## Subjects

The ten cores of `examples/rad`, written by `scripts/make_core_specs.py` from `b1d31ed` and unchanged since: `rad-core-1-18`, `-10`, `-12-18`, `-17-18`, `-33`, `-45`, `-55`, `-61`, `-65` and `-68`. A core example keeps every assumption of `rad` and cuts its guarantees to the core plus every non-weakenable guarantee. `rad`'s spec has not changed since `4ccc04e`, the commit the cores were extracted from, so the indices still hold.

The two Lift-Plus-Cruise cores of `b1d31ed` are left out. Both whole Lift-Plus-Cruise specs repair directly at this budget: 89 of 90 `lift-plus-cruise-full` runs finished inside the cap. `ventilator-controller` has no confirmed core, so it is not a subject.

## Arms and budget

The arms, the budget and the config tree are those of `2026-11-05-fretish-order`, generated by the same `configs` line: 16 arms of sweep O under `nsga2-apportion` and `base` and `only-climb` under `random`. The budget is 1000 bred individuals at population 100, with a 7200 s cap. 10 cores × 18 arms × 30 seeds gives 5,400 runs at 15 jobs a host.

## Host split

The campaign was first launched split by seed, with seeds 0–14 on av2 and 15–29 on av3. It was re-declared on 2026-11-09, part way through, to split by core instead. av2 runs every seed of `rad-core-1-18`, `-17-18`, `-33` and `-65` (2,160 runs), and av3 every seed of `rad-core-10`, `-12-18`, `-45`, `-55`, `-61` and `-68` (3,240 runs).

The reason is the pooled pass after this campaign. Each core's pool needs all 30 seeds of that core. When one host holds every seed of a core, that host can pool, fingerprint and compare the core after its own run phase ends, from the queue, with nobody at a terminal. Under the seed split, every core needed a collect to the local machine, a local pool and a commit before the pooled pass could start.

Each host keeps the runs it finished under the seed split. On its own cores, resume skips them. The other host's cores, at the seeds this host ran first, stay in its results directory as orphans: seeds 0–14 on av2 and 15–29 on av3. Runs are deterministic in the seed, so an orphan and the matching run on the other host carry the same search. Only the wall-time columns differ. When `collect` merges the run CSVs, the kept row for such a (spec, arm, seed) can come from either host, and only its wall time depends on which.

Curves are different. A curve embeds wall times too, and `collect --curves` refuses a run whose curves from two hosts are not byte-identical. So each score phase names its host's cores in `specs`, and the orphans are never scored.

The arms of one (core, seed) cell still run on one host, so a host difference still cancels inside each cell. Contrasts within a core are unaffected. Contrasts pooled over cores mix the hosts, as they did before.

## Measures

Cores carry no ideals, so `implies_ideal` is not read. The measures are found-repair rate, maximal repairs per run, wall time and the anytime curves of the score phase. Strength and diversity come from the pooled pass that follows this campaign.

## Analysis

All contrasts pair by (core, seed). Found-repair rate uses the exact McNemar test; wall time and repair count take the Hodges–Lehmann shift with the exact Wilcoxon signed-rank interval. RQ1 is `full` against `base`, `full` and `base` each against random `base`, and random `only-climb` against random `base` and against `full`. RQ2 is each `only-<f>` against `base` and each `no-<f>` against `full`, with a Holm correction over the seven features in each direction. Results are reported per core first, then pooled over the ten.

## Threats

- A repaired core is not a repaired `rad`. The other guarantees stay as they were, and nothing here checks the core's repair against them.
- One-guarantee cores (`rad-core-10`, `-33`, `-45`, `-55`, `-61`, `-65`, `-68`) can be weakened but never removed, because the search never removes the last live guarantee.
- `rad-core-10` is an outlier in both earlier campaigns: directed mutation found a repair on 9 of 30 seeds and uniform redraw on 30 of 30. It is reported on its own as well as in the pool.

## Cost

The 2026-10 rerun ran the cores at 9–65 s a run at parallel 1, 296 s summed over the ten per (arm, seed). The small set of `2026-11-05-fretish-order` ran at 1.09 times its rerun time and at 6.85 s of host wall a run at 15 jobs. That puts the run phase at about 3 h and the score phase at about 3 h.
